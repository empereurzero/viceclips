Clipper

The personal clip editing and publishing tool of ViceClips, a gaming channel.

Clipper is a private tool that runs on the creator's own computer. It turns the creator's own gameplay recordings (and videos the creator is authorized to use) into short vertical clips with subtitles, then publishes them to the creator's own accounts on YouTube, TikTok, Facebook and Instagram, using each platform's official API.

  • It is used only by the owner of the connected accounts. There is no public sign-up.
  • It has no server: all data stays on the creator's computer.
  • It never sells or shares data, shows no ads and contains no tracking.

Platforms used

  • YouTube (YouTube Data API): upload Shorts to the creator's channel and read their view counts.
  • TikTok (Login Kit, Content Posting API): publish videos to the creator's account and read their view counts.
  • Facebook and Instagram (Graph API): publish Reels to the creator's Page and professional Instagram account and read their view counts.
