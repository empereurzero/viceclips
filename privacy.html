Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 18, 2026

This policy explains how Clipper, the personal tool of the ViceClips gaming channel (“we”), handles data. Clipper is used only by the owner of the connected accounts to publish his own content. It runs entirely on his own computer and has no server.

1. Data we access

Only after the account owner signs in and explicitly authorizes it, Clipper accesses:

  • Google / YouTube (scopes youtube.upload and youtube.readonly): the channel ID, name and picture; the ability to upload videos to that channel; the view counts of the videos it uploaded.
  • TikTok (scopes user.info.basic, video.publish, video.list): the account's open ID, display name and avatar; the ability to publish videos; the view counts of published videos.
  • Meta (Facebook Pages and Instagram professional accounts): the list, names and access tokens of the Pages the owner manages, the linked Instagram account ID and username; the ability to publish Reels; the view counts of published Reels.

Clipper does not access emails, contacts, messages, payment data or the data of any other user.

2. How we use it

This data is used only to publish the creator's own clips to his own accounts at the scheduled times and to show him how many views they get. It is never used for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose.

3. Storage and security

Everything is stored locally on the creator's computer. Access tokens are encrypted with the Windows Data Protection API, so they can only be read by the creator's own Windows session. Data is sent only to the platform it belongs to (for example, a video for YouTube is sent only to YouTube), over encrypted HTTPS connections.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, share or transfer any data to third parties. There are no analytics, trackers or ads.

5. Google API Services

Clipper's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Clipper uses YouTube API Services: see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while an account stays connected. Removing an account in Clipper deletes its token. You can also revoke access at any time:

  • Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions
  • TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions
  • Meta: Facebook Settings → Apps and websites

To request deletion of any data, write to empereursajanthan@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days.

7. Children

Clipper is not directed to children and is not used by anyone other than the account owner.

8. Changes and contact

We will update this page if anything changes. Contact: empereursajanthan@gmail.com
